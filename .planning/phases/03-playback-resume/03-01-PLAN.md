<user_story>
**As a** listener with an imported audiobook library,
**I want to** tap a book and have it play, and have the app remember exactly where I stopped,
**so that** I can close the app, come back days later, and pick up where I left off.

*Sourced by reformatting the ROADMAP Phase 3 `**Goal:**` line ("User can play any imported audiobook with full transport controls, and playback always resumes exactly where they left off — delivering the app's core value") into the three-part story shape. No actor, capability, or benefit was invented — if a canonical story is wanted, run `/gsd mvp-phase 3`.*
</user_story>

<objective>
Prove the entire playback architecture end-to-end on one path: tap a library row, land on a dedicated player route, press play, hear audio, have the position persist, and have it restored after a full app relaunch.

This is the phase's tracer slice. It deliberately carries the riskiest constraints in the whole phase — the iOS gesture-unlock rule, the object-URL lifecycle against a live query that re-fires every 5 seconds, and the position-write cost against a record that co-locates a multi-hundred-MB Blob — so that an architectural dead-end surfaces after one commit rather than after the scrub bar, skip buttons, and time readout are already built on top of it.

Purpose: PLAY-01, PLAY-05, and PLAY-06 are the app's stated Core Value. Nothing else in this phase matters if this slice does not hold.
Output: A working `/player/[id]` route with play/pause and durable resume, plus the DOM-free playback predicate module the rest of the phase builds on.
</objective>
