<user_story>
**As a** listener partway through an audiobook,
**I want to** see how far in I am and how much is left, and jump back 15 seconds when I miss a line,
**so that** I can follow the book without losing my place when my attention drifts.

*Expansion slice of the Phase 3 story framed in `03-01-PLAN.md`. Sourced from the ROADMAP Phase 3 `**Goal:**` line; no actor, capability, or benefit invented.*
</user_story>

<objective>
Expand the proven player slice with the two things a listener reaches for constantly: knowing where they are in the book, and jumping back over a sentence they missed.

Both tasks lead with a pure, mechanically tested function and then wire it into the screen, following the convention `lib/format.ts` already established in Phase 2 — the boundary rules that are easy to get subtly wrong (rounding across the hour boundary, clamping at the start and the end of the book) get real `node --test` coverage instead of a visual spot-check.

Purpose: PLAY-04 makes progress legible; PLAY-02 makes the book navigable. D-04's end-of-book terminal state also lands here, because it is the same clamp boundary seen from the other side.
Output: A player screen with a live elapsed/remaining readout, working ±15s transport, and an honest completed state.
</objective>
